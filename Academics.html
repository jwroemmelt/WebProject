What You Can Learn

At St. John's University, you will find a wide range of academic programs designed to challenge and engage you. Our faculty members are experts in their fields and are dedicated to providing you with a high-quality education.

Computer Science

Our Computer Science program provides students with a strong foundation in programming, algorithms, and software engineering. You will have the opportunity to work on real-world projects and collaborate with faculty members on cutting-edge research.

Business Administration

Our Business Administration program prepares students for leadership roles in the business world. You will learn about finance, marketing, management, and entrepreneurship, and gain practical experience through internships and case studies.

Health Sciences

Our Health Sciences program offers students the opportunity to explore careers in healthcare, public health, and biomedical research. You will gain hands-on experience in clinical settings and learn from experienced professionals in the field.

Liberal Arts

Our Liberal Arts program encourages students to explore a wide range of subjects, including literature, history, philosophy, and the arts. You will develop critical thinking skills and gain a deeper understanding of the world around you.
